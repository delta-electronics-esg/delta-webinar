About this webinar

Most sites run meters and equipment from several brands, so energy data ends up in spreadsheets and ISO 50001 savings are hard to prove. Dayann Charles (Verdantix) and Karthik Arunachalam (Delta) discuss how to unify that data, run the PDCA cycle on it, and use AI to find and verify savings.
